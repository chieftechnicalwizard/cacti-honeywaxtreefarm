Nomenclature & Cross-Notation Guide: In standard Trichocereus breeding notation (Mother × Father), the left parent is the seed-bearing mother plant and the right parent is the pollen donor. Note that (Plantas s agradas. A Rossi) in the original listing is a parenthetical note identifying P.S.A. as Spanish breeder Andrea Rossi’s nursery (Planta S. Agradas / Plantas Sagradas), not an 18th cross. Also note the crucial botanical distinction between true Ogun (T. pachanoi, Matucana) and KT-Ogun (Keeper Trout’s intensely glaucous T. peruvianus).
